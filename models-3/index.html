Pretrained models

Recommended Models

For CO2 and H2O adsorption: Use UMA, trained on all FAIR chemistry datasets including ODAC25.

For N2 and O2 adsorption: Use the dedicated eSEN models trained on ODAC25, as UMA was not trained on these adsorbates.

ODAC25 Models¶

As part of the ODAC25 release, we released two sets of models:

  1. UMA models trained on a range of FAIR chemistry datasets including a subset of ODAC25, available at the UMA HuggingFace site

  2. eSEN models trained only on ODAC25, available at the ODAC25 HuggingFace site

The UMA models were only trained on CO₂ and H₂O adsorbates, and are competitive with the eSEN models for these adsorbates. Since the UMA models were not trained on N₂ and O₂, we strongly recommend using the eSEN models for these.
